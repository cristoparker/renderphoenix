**Moon Forces** is an interactive Moon colony building and resource management simulation created for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025** under the prompt *“Your Home in Space: The Habitat Layout Creator.”*

Developed in roughly four to five days by **Team Space Kitty** (connecting members of RenderPhoenix), the project earned **2nd Runner-Up in the Dhaka Division** (among 850+ participants at BASIS) and advanced to become an official **NASA Space Apps 2025 Global Nominee**.

### Achievement
**Global Nominee — NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025**  
*Dhaka Division 2nd Runner-Up (BASIS)*

![Moon Forces Logo & Title Banner](/assets/images/projects/moon-forces/Moon%20Forces%20Nasa%20Space%20App%20Challenge%202025%20logo%20banner.png)

### Core Gameplay & Systems

Moon Forces challenges players to establish, power, and sustain a modular human settlement on the lunar surface under strict resource constraints:

- **Modular Construction Grid**: Choose build sites across the lunar regolith and construct habitat pods, solar farms, greenhouses, and life support nodes.
- **Intertwined Resource Economy**: Balance 8 dynamic resources: Oxygen, Water, Food, Crops, Electricity, Waste/Trash, Metal, and Crew Population. Expanding infrastructure increases power and maintenance loads.
- **Day/Night Cycle & Power Storage**: Solar panels produce energy during the 180-second lunar day, requiring players to build battery reserves before the cold, dark lunar night halts solar generation.
- **Autonomous Rovers**: NavMesh-powered ground rovers traverse pathways between domes, collecting samples and bringing the settlement to life.
- **Dynamic Space Flights**: Scheduled supply rockets arrive from Earth and launch back into orbit.

### Technical Specifications

- **Game Engine**: Unity
- **Scripting & Logic**: C# & Playmaker visual state machines
- **Visuals & Shaders**: ShaderLab / HLSL custom surface shaders
- **3D Asset Modeling**: Low-poly modular assets modeled in Blender
- **Pathfinding**: Unity NavMesh Agent Navigation
