# Banglamine City 2 – Bangladesh Minecraft City Map

**Banglamine City 2** is a large-scale fictional Minecraft city map inspired by the architecture, streets, infrastructure, landmarks, and everyday urban environments of Bangladesh.

Originally developed around 2020, Banglamine City 2 brings a distinctly Bangladeshi atmosphere into Minecraft through detailed buildings, busy streets, local markets, railway infrastructure, public spaces, residential neighborhoods, and surrounding rural environments.

The map is available for **Minecraft Java Edition and Minecraft Bedrock Edition** and is designed for exploration, creative gameplay, roleplay, multiplayer experiences, Minecraft videos, and storytelling.

## About Banglamine City 2

Banglamine City 2 is a fictional city inspired by real Bangladeshi environments.

The city is not intended to be a 1:1 recreation of Dhaka or any other specific city. Instead, it combines architectural styles, infrastructure, streetscapes, monuments, markets, neighborhoods, and environmental elements commonly found across Bangladesh into one large Minecraft world.

The result is a Minecraft city environment where players can explore dense urban neighborhoods, commercial areas, railway infrastructure, public spaces, landmarks, and countryside surrounding the city.

Banglamine City 2 expands on the original Banglamine City project with a larger and more developed environment, providing more locations to explore and more possibilities for creative gameplay and storytelling.
