**Frostcraft** is a frozen steampunk survival colony map inspired by the acclaimed society survival game *Frostpunk*. Set in a brutal sub-zero wasteland crater, the settlement is built around a massive central coal-powered generator designed to keep the last remnants of civilization warm against an unforgiving eternal winter.

### The Frozen Colony & Architecture

The settlement is arranged in concentric circular rings designed to maximize the thermal zone provided by the central tower:

- **Central Thermal Generator**: The monumental steam-and-coal furnace standing at the core of the city, complete with exhaust chimneys, support struts, and heat distribution channels.
- **Colony Infrastructure**: Ringed zones of survival shelters, medical tents, coal storage, watchtowers, and a central church.
- **Steampunk Automatons & Scouts**: Massive mechanical walkers striding across the snow-covered perimeter alongside tethered reconnaissance balloons.
- **Mountain Ridge Outpost**: A cliffside staging camp situated along the crater ridge overlooking the frosty basin.
