Frostcraft was built with inspiration from frostpunk survival aesthetics. Players must manage warmth, navigate severe blizzard environments, and shelter inside a central thermal generator outpost.

### Highlights
- **Sub-zero Settlement**: Concentric circular city layout surrounding a massive central heat generator core.
- **Extreme Weather Atmosphere**: Custom frost terrain, frozen water bodies, and snowed-in structures.
