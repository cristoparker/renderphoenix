**Villager News Vehicle Addon** brings the funny and crazy vehicles from the legendary *Villager News* series into Minecraft Bedrock! Villagers have built their own vehicles, and they are ready to cause some chaos. Drive, fly, and explore your world with a full fleet of villager-made machines — including the legendary **Daladas** fighter plane!

![youtube:Villager News Vehicle Addon Showcase](https://youtu.be/3ob-w_OtGb0)

[Join our Discord Server](https://discord.gg/H5SwpEM4Ar) to follow development, updates, and community feedback.

---

## Included Vehicles

- **Daladas** — The legendary fighter plane from Villager News. Soar through the skies with one of the most famous villager vehicles ever created!
- **Villager Helicopter** — Take to the sky with a functional helicopter constructed by villagers.
- **Villager Tank** — Roll out the villager army's heavy-duty armored tank to defend your village.
- **Villager Firefighter Car** — Help the villager firefighters respond to red-hot emergencies with their sirens and special vehicle.
- **Villager Boat** — Cruise across rivers and oceans in a hilarious villager-made boat ("*I am a boat!*").

*More vehicles and features are planned for future updates!*
