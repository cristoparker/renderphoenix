**FunMOBA PVP** is a fast-paced 3v3 and 4v4 team-based base defense PvP battleground inspired by classic MOBA titles. Built completely in vanilla Minecraft Bedrock using optimized command blocks, it offers a lag-free, tactical objective-driven alternative to traditional Bedwars and Skywars.

### Arena & Map Layout

The arena features two symmetrical mirrored bases (Red vs. Cyan) connected by outer lanes and a dense center jungle divided by a river with strategic bridge crossings.

![FunMOBA Arena Map Layout](/assets/images/projects/funmoba-pvp/Renderphoenix%20Minecraft%20%20funmoba-pvp-moba-style-team-pvp_2.png)

### Class Roles & Kits

Players can select from 4 specialized combat roles before heading into battle:

- **Tank**: High survivability and damage absorption equipped with a knockback shield and sword. Excels at crowd control and countering Archers and Assassins.
- **Archer (Marksman)**: High ranged DPS and knockback capabilities. Can snipe and damage the enemy Nexus from a safe distance without triggering blindness debuffs.
- **Fighter**: High sustained melee damage output. The primary counter against heavy Tanks and lethal when closing the distance against ranged heroes.
- **Assassin**: Extreme mobility with super speed and high leap mechanics. Deals high burst damage and burn effects, ideal for surprise ambushes and backdoor Nexus pushes.

### How to Play & Objectives

1. **Lobby Match Setup**: Players spawn in the staging lobby where operators or players can split into Red and Cyan teams and start the match with a single click.
2. **Lane Pushing & Jungle Control**: Teams push forward along lanes or flank through the jungle river to bypass enemy defenses.
3. **Nexus Assault**: Each team has a central Nexus core with 100 HP. Strike the core button to deal 3 damage per hit.
4. **Victory Condition**: The live sidebar scoreboard tracks both Nexus health pools in real time. The first team to reduce the opponent's Nexus to 0 claims victory.
