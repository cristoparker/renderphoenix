**Better Physics** improves mob physics in Minecraft Bedrock Edition, making mobs move, fall, and react more naturally.

Better Physics is a Minecraft Bedrock Edition addon that improves mob physics to make the game feel more realistic and immersive. Experience mobs with improved physical behavior and enjoy a fresh way to explore your Minecraft world.

Whether you're exploring, building, or surviving, Better Physics adds a new touch to your gameplay.

## Features

- **Improved mob physics** — mobs move, fall, and react more naturally.
- **More immersive gameplay** — a fresh, realistic feel to every encounter.
- **Designed for Minecraft Bedrock Edition** — built for Bedrock, no external tooling required.

## A More Natural World

Mob movement is one of the most constant parts of a Minecraft session, so even small improvements to how creatures walk, fall, and react change the feel of the entire game. Better Physics leans into that by smoothing out the physical behavior you see every time you explore, build, or survive.
