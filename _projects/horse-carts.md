**Horse Carts Addon** makes your Minecraft adventures more fun! Explore the world with different horse carts, each featuring its own unique look and purpose. From carrying items to farming and battle-themed carts, there is a whole new layer of mobile utility to discover.

More carts may be added in future updates.

![youtube:Horse Carts Addon Showcase](https://youtu.be/Im0x9x4qd6Q)

[Join our Discord Server](https://discord.gg/H5SwpEM4Ar) to follow development, updates, and community feedback.

---

## Available Carts

- **Horse Cart** — A simple cart for your adventures.
- **Brewery Horse Cart** — A cart equipped with a brewing station and barrels.
- **Covered Horse Cart** — A cart with a roof to cover its cargo.
- **Forge Horse Cart** — A cart equipped with a forge and smelter for crafting.
- **Harvest Horse Cart** — A cart with a farming theme.
- **Jail Horse Cart** — A cart with a jail-style design.
- **Storage Horse Cart** — A cart designed for carrying items.
- **TNT Horse Cart** — A cart with a TNT theme.
- **War Horse Cart** — A cart with a battle-ready design.

## Features

- Different horse cart designs tailored for exploration and survival
- Utility carts for many kinds of adventures (crafting, storage, farming, and combat)
