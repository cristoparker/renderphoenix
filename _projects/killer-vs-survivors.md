**Killer vs Survivors** is an asymmetrical 4v1 survival horror minigame built for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Inspired by intense hide-and-seek and survival games, players are pitted against each other in a deadly game of cat and mouse inside the gloomy estate of a psychopath killer.

The map is engineered purely with lightweight vanilla Bedrock command blocks and scoreboard systems—requiring **no external resource packs or add-ons**. It is fully plug-and-play across Minecraft Realms, dedicated BDS servers, and local LAN sessions.

### The Dark Mansion Arena

The match takes place within the claustrophobic grounds of a foreboding mansion estate. Dim torchlight and glowing lanterns barely pierce the darkness, casting long shadows across winding cobblestone pathways and overgrown foliage where danger lurks around every corner.

![Dark Mansion Courtyard and Overgrown Pathways](/assets/images/projects/killer-vs-survivor/Renderphoenix%20Minecraft%20killer-vs-survivors-minigame_2.png)

### Roles & Asymmetrical Balance

The game requires at least 4 survivors (5+ players recommended) and 1 killer:

- **Survivors (Unarmed Prey)**:
  - Spawn with no weapons or armor.
  - Must navigate the perilous terrain, locate coin generators, and collect coins to unlock the exit.
  - Can stage daring rescue missions to break captured teammates out of the Killer's Cage.
- **The Killer (Empowered Hunter)**:
  - Equipped with full netherite armor and devastating weapons.
  - Survivors cannot kill the killer.
  - Primary goal is to stalk the shadows, hunt down all survivors, and lock them away before they can escape.
  - Restricted from scoring coins, entering the survivor safe zone, or camping inside the cage.

### Core Mechanics & Objectives

1. **Scoreboard Coin Generators**: Three interactive coin generators are hidden across the mansion grounds. Survivors must locate and activate them to build up their team's coin tally tracked on the live sidebar scoreboard.
2. **The Killer's Cage & Teammate Rescues**: When a survivor is struck down, they are banished to the Killer's Cage. Any active survivor can rescue their fallen teammate at the cost of 50 coins.
3. **The Escape Door & Safe Zone**: The ultimate objective for survivors is to gather 50 coins and reach the designated safe zone—a protected sanctuary paved with emerald ore located at the spawn chamber. Unlocking the escape door seals victory for the survivors.

![The Killer's Cage Respawn Area and Rescue Station](/assets/images/projects/killer-vs-survivor/Renderphoenix%20Minecraft%20%20killer-vs-survivors-minigame_3.png)

### Game Lobby & Host Controls

The dedicated staging lobby provides an intuitive control room with both operator host permissions and player self-control features. Hosts can seamlessly reset the game state, clear scoreboards, teleport players, and initiate matches with single-button triggers.

![Game Staging Lobby and Host Controls](/assets/images/projects/killer-vs-survivor/Renderphoenix%20Minecraft%20killer-vs-survivors-minigame_5.png)

### How to Play & Match Rules

- **Recommended Players:** 5 players (1 Killer vs 4 Survivors).
- **Survivor Victory:** Collect 50 coins through generators, keep your teammates rescued, and escape through the emerald safe zone door.
- **Killer Victory:** Eliminate all survivors and secure every player inside the cage before the escape threshold is achieved.
- **Server Compatibility:** Fully functional on Minecraft Bedrock Realms, dedicated BDS servers, and local LAN.
