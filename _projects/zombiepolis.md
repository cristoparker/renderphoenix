Zombiepolis is a detailed post-apocalyptic city map designed for survival gameplay and atmospheric storytelling. Features overgrown skyscrapers, abandoned highways, ruined storefronts, and barricaded safehouses.

### Key Features
- **Atmospheric Worldbuilding**: Environmental storytelling with collapsed bridges, rusted vehicles, and decaying structures.
- **Survival Ready**: Optimized for survival multiplayer setups, mob encounters, and custom story campaigns.
- **Detailed Districts**: Includes downtown, industrial sector, hospital grounds, and subterranean subway tunnels.
