**Zombiepolis** is a detailed post-apocalyptic coastal city map created for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Designed specifically as an optimized, lag-free survival environment for mobile (PE), console, and PC players, it pushes the boundaries of atmospheric environmental storytelling in Bedrock.

The map provides an immersive sandbox for custom zombie apocalypse survival campaigns, PvP tournaments, hide-and-seek matches, and Hunger Games events.

![youtube:Zombiepolis - A Post Apocalyptic City Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EIj7E9zgtg8)

### The Coastal Metropolis & Crash Sites

Set on a self-contained custom island bordered by rocky mountains, open plains, dense woodlands, and sandy coastlines, the urban center of Zombiepolis has been completely reclaimed by nature and catastrophe:

- **Stranded Cargo Freighter**: A massive multi-colored container ship run aground along the city waterfront, filled with lootable shipping crates.
- **High-Rise Airliner Collision**: A commercial passenger jetliner lodged directly into the upper floors of an apartment complex.
- **Overgrown Boulevards**: Wide multi-lane roadways covered in cracked asphalt, derelict vehicles, traffic blockades, and creeping ivy.

### The Medical Center & City Hospital

At the heart of the city stands a massive multi-story medical complex serving as a prime high-risk, high-reward scavenging zone:

- **Hospital Facilities**: Complete with emergency ambulance bays, a rooftop helipad, and a public transit bus hub.
- **Communications Infrastructure**: Tall red-and-white broadcast towers providing vantage points across the skyline.
- **Urban Decay**: Abandoned multi-story residential blocks with shattered windows and overgrown foliage.

![City Hospital Complex and Transit Station](/assets/images/projects/zombiepolis/Renderphoenix%20Minecraft%20zombiepolis--a-post-apocalyptic-city_2.webp)

### Survival Rules & Urban Scavenging

Zombiepolis is balanced to deliver a genuine scavenger survival experience:

1. **Safe Starting Outpost**: Players spawn in a secure shelter stocked with a bed and a starter survival kit before stepping out into the ruins.
2. **Urban Scavenging Focus**: Traditional underground mining is disabled; players must explore apartment rooms, warehouses, and vehicle trunks across the island to find food, weapons, and medical supplies.
3. **Island Boundary**: The playable area is confined to the island perimeter, ensuring fast-paced encounters and tight multiplayer pacing.

### Recommended Add-ons & Mod Setup

To get the most intense survival horror atmosphere out of Zombiepolis, we recommend pairing the map with:

- **Daylight Zombie Burn Prevention**: Add-ons that stop undead mobs from igniting in the sunlight.
- **Apocalyptic Shaders & Fog**: Visual packs with desaturated foliage textures, overcast weather, and dense fog shaders.
- **Firearms & Military Vehicles**: Gun add-ons, sniper rifles, and vehicle packs to fight off roaming hordes.
- **Mutant Creature Packs**: Custom mutant bosses and enhanced mob behavior packs for greater combat variety.
