## Our Story

RenderPhoenix was originally established in **2019** under the name **Minehutt** by **Tasrif Ibn Mizan**. What began as an independent creative experiment crafting virtual worlds quickly expanded into a recognized community presence through projects such as *Banglamine City*, *Zombiepolis*, vehicle add-ons, and customized UI packs.

On **January 30, 2021**, the studio officially adopted the name **RenderPhoenix** to reflect its evolving visual identity, technical ambition, and focus on high-quality 3D asset creation.

### Resilience Through Challenges

On **1 August 2022**, RenderPhoenix faced a major setback when several of its core digital accounts and communication channels were compromised. Despite this disruption, the studio continued operating and publishing content until 2024, when the original team gradually dispersed into university and software development careers.

### The 2026 Revival

On **22 August 2026**, **Cristo Parker** officially initiated the revival of RenderPhoenix. Rebuilding the studio's digital infrastructure and reassembling team capabilities, RenderPhoenix is now expanding beyond its historical roots into full-scale game development with Unreal Engine 5, Unity, Blender, and original IP creation.

## Studio Timeline

- **2019 — Minehutt Founded:** Tasrif Ibn Mizan establishes Minehutt, publishing early community maps and city builds on MCPEDL.
- **2021 — Renamed to RenderPhoenix:** Adopts the RenderPhoenix identity to signal a commitment to 3D world creation and technical design.
- **1 August 2022 — Account Compromise & Resilience:** Overcomes major security breaches across core social channels, continuing project releases through 2024.
- **2024 — Period of Inactivity:** The original team gradually disperses; studio operations pause while members pursue higher education.
- **2025 — NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025:** RenderPhoenix achieves Regional Runner-Up with a Moon settlement interactive simulator.
- **22 August 2026 — Official Revival Initiated:** Cristo Parker initiates the official revival, transitioning RenderPhoenix into games, 3D worlds, and Unreal/Unity production.

- **Cristo Parker (Manager):** Spearheaded the studio's official revival on 22 August 2026, overseeing technical architecture, systems development, and strategic direction.
