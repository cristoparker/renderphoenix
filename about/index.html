--- layout: page title: "About RenderPhoenix" description: "The history, people and direction of RenderPhoenix — an independent creative studio from Bangladesh, founded in 2019." ---

RenderPhoenix started in 2019 as Minehutt, founded by Tasrif Ibn Mizan. The early team hosted Minecraft realms, played with the community and built custom adventure maps.

On 27 January 2021 the studio became RenderPhoenix, reflecting a sharper visual identity and a focus on high-quality 3D work. Under the new name it published its best-known community projects on MCPEDL — Banglamine City, Zombiepolis, vehicle add-ons like the DHK Rickshaw and Phoenix Bicycle, and custom UI packs.

On 1 August 2022 several core accounts were compromised. The studio kept publishing until 2024, when the original team moved on to university and software careers.

On 22 August 2026 RenderPhoenix began its revival — rebuilding its infrastructure and expanding into full game development with Unreal Engine 5, Unity, Blender and original IP.
