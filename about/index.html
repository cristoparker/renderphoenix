Our Story

RenderPhoenix was originally established in 2019 under the name Minehutt by Tasrif Ibn Mizan. In the early Minehutt days, the team hosted Minecraft realms, played together with community members, and had fun making custom adventure maps.

On January 30, 2021, the studio officially adopted the name RenderPhoenix to reflect its evolving visual identity, technical ambition, and focus on high-quality 3D asset creation. Under the RenderPhoenix banner, the studio created and published iconic community projects on MCPEDL, including Banglamine City, Zombiepolis, custom vehicle add-ons (such as the DHK Rickshaw and Phoenix Bicycle), and customized UI packs.

Resilience Through Challenges

On 1 August 2022, RenderPhoenix faced a major setback when several of its core digital accounts and communication channels were compromised. Despite this disruption, the studio continued operating and publishing content until 2024, when the original team gradually dispersed into university and software development careers.

The 2026 Revival

On 22 August 2026, RenderPhoenix officially initiated its studio revival. Rebuilding the studio's digital infrastructure and reassembling team capabilities, RenderPhoenix is now expanding beyond its historical roots into full-scale game development with Unreal Engine 5, Unity, Blender, and original IP creation.
