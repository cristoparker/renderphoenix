# DMCA & Copyright Policy

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 25, 2026

RenderPhoenix Creatives ("RenderPhoenix", "we", "us", or "our") respects the intellectual property and copyright rights of creators, developers, artists, publishers, and other rights holders.

RenderPhoenix is an independent creative studio based in Tejgaon, Dhaka, Bangladesh. Our website may contain original RenderPhoenix content, contributor-submitted content, and links or embeds to third-party platforms.

---

## Copyright Infringement

If you believe that copyrighted material available on the RenderPhoenix website infringes your copyright or the copyright of someone you are authorized to represent, please contact us.

Send copyright and DMCA-related requests to:

**Email:** [contact@renderphoenix.com](mailto:contact@renderphoenix.com)

Please include enough information for us to identify and review the material, including:

- Your full name and contact information.
- Identification of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
- The URL or location of the allegedly infringing material on our website.
- A description explaining why you believe the material infringes your copyright.
- A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or applicable law.
- A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
- Your electronic or physical signature.

## Review and Removal

After receiving a sufficiently detailed copyright complaint, RenderPhoenix may review the reported material and take appropriate action.

Depending on the circumstances, we may:

- Remove or disable access to the reported content.
- Remove a download or external link.
- Contact the relevant contributor or content owner.
- Request additional information or evidence of ownership.
- Decline to remove content where the complaint is incomplete, unsupported, authorized, or otherwise not sufficient to establish infringement.

A copyright complaint does not automatically mean that infringement has occurred. We may consider applicable licenses, permissions, fair use, fair dealing, public-domain status, commentary, criticism, and other applicable legal exceptions.

## Contributor Content

Some content published by RenderPhoenix may be submitted by independent contributors.

Contributors are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights, permissions, or licenses to submit material for publication.

Submitting content to RenderPhoenix does not automatically transfer copyright ownership to RenderPhoenix.

If a contributor's work is reported for copyright infringement, we may contact the contributor and request relevant information or evidence.

## Third-Party Platforms

RenderPhoenix may link to or redirect visitors to external platforms such as GitHub, MCPEDL, Steam, Google Play, itch.io, YouTube, and other services.

If the disputed material is hosted directly by a third-party platform, the copyright owner may also need to submit a complaint to that platform.

RenderPhoenix does not control the infrastructure, content, copyright procedures, or policies of external platforms.

Removing a link from RenderPhoenix does not necessarily remove the material from an external platform.
