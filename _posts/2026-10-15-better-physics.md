**Better Physics** improves mob physics in Minecraft Bedrock Edition, making mobs move, fall, and react more naturally.

Better Physics is a Minecraft Bedrock Edition addon that improves mob physics to make the game feel more realistic and immersive. Experience mobs with improved physical behavior and enjoy a fresh way to explore your Minecraft world.

Whether you're exploring, building, or surviving, Better Physics adds a new touch to your gameplay.

## Features

- **Improved mob physics** — mobs move, fall, and interact with the world in a more believable way.
- **More immersive gameplay** — natural motion makes every encounter feel grounded.
- **Designed for Minecraft Bedrock Edition** — built natively for Bedrock, no external mods required.

## A More Natural World

Mob movement is one of the most constant things you experience in Minecraft, so even small improvements to how creatures fall, walk, and react can change the feel of the entire game. Better Physics leans into that idea by smoothing out the physical behavior you see every session.
