## Studio Capabilities Overview

RenderPhoenix bridges technical programming, 3D world design, and creative art direction to craft interactive experiences, real-time assets, and complete games.

---

### 1. Game Development
From gameplay mechanics and systems architecture to character control and physics tuning, RenderPhoenix crafts immersive games using industry-standard game engines.

- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Gameplay Systems Programming
  - Level Architecture & Mechanics
  - Cross-platform Optimization
  - Physics & Player Controllers
- **Technologies:** Unreal Engine, Unity, C#, C++

---

### 2. 3D Environments & World Building
Creating game-ready environments, architectural layouts, post-apocalyptic zones, and fantasy terrain with a focus on lighting, spatial storytelling, and memory efficiency.

- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Environment Art & Layout
  - Modular City Systems
  - Lighting & Atmospheric FX
  - Low/High-poly Modeling & Texturing
- **Technologies:** Blender, Unreal Engine 5, Substance Painter

---

### 3. Game & Production Assets
Creating asset packs, vehicles, props, customized UI themes, and modular components optimized for integration into games and interactive simulations.

- **Key Capabilities:**
  - 3D Asset Production & Rigging
  - Real-Time Vehicle Add-Ons
  - Custom Game UI & HUD Design
  - Texture & Material Authoring
- **Technologies:** Blender, Blockbench, Photoshop, Figma

---

### 4. Technical Art & Shaders
Optimizing real-time visuals, writing custom HLSL/GLSL shaders, establishing production art pipelines, and balancing aesthetic beauty with high framerates.

- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Custom Shaders & FX
  - LOD & Mesh Optimization
  - VFX & Particle Systems
  - Technical Art Pipeline Design
- **Technologies:** HLSL / GLSL, Node Shaders, Unreal Material Editor

---

### 5. Digital Experiences & Creative Tech
Exploring the intersection of science, interactivity, and creative code—such as lunar exploration simulations and custom digital tools.

- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Rapid Prototyping
  - Scientific & Simulation Projects
  - Interactive Web & UI
  - Hackathon & Challenge Builds
- **Technologies:** Web3D / Three.js, Game Engines, Interactive Tooling
