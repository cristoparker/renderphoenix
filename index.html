Studio Identity

An Indie Creative Studio

We started back in 2019 by hosting Minecraft realms, crafting custom worlds, and making fun add-ons for players. Today, RenderPhoenix creates original indie games, 3D models, and interactive digital experiences.

What began as playing together with friends and building maps has grown into an indie game studio. We use tools like Unreal Engine and Blender to bring our ideas to life. Our goal is simple: to make fun, beautiful worlds and games that anyone can jump into and enjoy.

Timeline

The Evolution of RenderPhoenix

Key milestones from our founding in 2019 to our modern interactive studio revival.

2019

Minehutt Founded

Tasrif Ibn Mizan establishes Minehutt, crafting community Minecraft maps, city builds, and add-ons.

2021

RenderPhoenix Identity

Officially re-branded as RenderPhoenix to represent transformation and high-quality 3D assets.

Aug 2022

Security Incident & Resilience

Faced a major account compromise; the team persevered and continued releases through 2024.

2025

NASA Space Apps Recognition

Achieved Regional Runner-Up at NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025 with lunar habitat simulator.

22 Aug 2026

Official Studio Revival

Studio revival initiated, expanding into Unreal Engine, Unity, Blender, and original IP development.
