Studio Origin

Resilience & Evolution

Founded by Tasrif Ibn Mizan, focusing on virtual city maps and community creations.

2021

RenderPhoenix Identity

Officially re-branded as RenderPhoenix to represent transformation and high-quality 3D assets.
